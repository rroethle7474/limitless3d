Quick answers

How fast will I hear back?
Usually the same day. This is a small shop, so messages go straight to the person doing the work.
Can I drop something off?
Yes. We are in Neenah and happy to look at the object in person. Get in touch first so we know you are coming.
Do you ship?
Parts from the shop ship nationwide. For custom work, you can ship the original to us and we will ship it back with the new part.
Do you take on big jobs?
We work with individuals through to large corporations. Tell us the scope and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right shop.
