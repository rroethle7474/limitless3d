# Parts-wall photo sources

Decorative product shots used in the parts-shop "door" section (`.wall` tiles). They are
`aria-hidden` / `alt=""` in the prototype — they carry no semantic content, hence the
positional names.

Originally **hotlinked** by the prototype from the old Square Online CDN at `?width=400`.
Downloaded here at full resolution and self-hosted per plan §6 (decision D-003).

Source pattern:
`https://140396716.cdn6.editmysite.com/uploads/1/4/0/3/140396716/s666696764460511456_<id>.jpeg`
